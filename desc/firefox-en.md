Transform the standard YouTube live chat into a dynamic, scrolling "bullet chat" (danmaku) system similar to niconico or bilibili.

*Note: This add-on is currently under development and may occasionally encounter issues. If you experience any problems, please try refreshing the page or switching to the mini-player.*

If you prefer to use an older version, please refer to the [Installation Guide (GitHub wiki)](https://github.com/ys-j/YoutubeLiveChatFlusher/wiki/Installation)


**Features**

- **Standalone**🌐: Enjoy bullet chat even when you have the side chat window closed. (*Available in Independent Mode*)
- **Mobile Friendly**📱: Watch livestreams with bullet chat on the go. (Works on YouTube's mobile site: `m.youtube.com`.)
- **Pinnable & Movable Messages**📌\*: Right-click any message that caught your eye to freeze it in place or move it where you want it.
- **Real-time Translation**🔠: Translate scrolling messages on the fly using your browser's built-in translator, external services (e.g., Google Translate), or even a Local LLM (OpenAI-compatible API).
- **Adjustable Replay (Time-shift)**⏳: Tweak the timing of chat replays for archived videos. (*Available in Independent Mode*)
- **Picture-in-Picture with Chat (Beta)**📺\*: Watch your favorite content and keep the scrolling chat visible in a dedicated PiP window.
- **Person Detection Support (Alpha)**✨\*: Focus on your fave by automatically detecting them and hiding messages behind them. *(As an AI-based feature, this is resource-intensive and may significantly impact device performance.)*

\*: Desktop-only


**Configurable Options** (Highlights)

- Animation Speed (Scrolling Speed)
- Font Size / Number of Lines / Line Height
- Font Family & Weight (CSS syntax, e.g., Roboto, "Noto Sans JP" / 500)
- Visibility and Color for: Member/Moderator/Owner/Verified user messages, Superchats, Superstickers/Gifts, New Memberships, and Milestones.
- Stroke Color and Width
- Flow Density & Direction
- Word Filter (Banned Words List)
- Time-shift Duration (in seconds)
- User-defined CSS
- Hotkeys (Keyboard Shortcuts)

Access the config panel via the "Gear" icon, similar to video quality or annotation settings.

Detailed Documentation: [Github wiki](https://github.com/ys-j/YoutubeLiveChatFlusher/wiki)
